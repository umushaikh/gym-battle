Settings

Used only in your browser to call Claude and generate ideas — never sent anywhere else, never stored on a server. Get a key at console.anthropic.com. Without a key, you'll get a starter pack of generic ideas instead of AI-generated ones.

Reminders

This app has no server — reminders fire locally in your browser: reliably when you open the app past your reminder time without having posted yet, and best-effort in the background if you've installed this to your home screen on Android/Chrome. iOS Safari can't fire background reminders for web apps, so opening the app is what triggers the nudge there.
